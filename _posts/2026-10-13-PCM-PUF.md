## A probabilistic circuit with phase change memory nanodevices & pass transistor logic selectors

Our paper **A probabilistic circuit with phase change memory nanodevices & pass transistor logic selectors** is now published in *Memories - Materials, Devices, Circuits and Systems*.

The study proposes two probabilistic circuit designs for physical unclonable functions using phase change memory nanodevices and pass transistor logic selectors. It combines circuit simulation, experimental validation, and machine learning analysis to investigate hardware security.

Co-authors: Sadid Muneer, Sameia Zaman, Md. Al Amin Ashik, Toufiq Mohammad Hasan, Fahim Hafiz, and Nafisa Noor.
