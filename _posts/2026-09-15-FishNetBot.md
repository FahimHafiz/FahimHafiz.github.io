## FishNetBot: An IoT and Deep Learning-Based Automated Fish Sorting Robot

Our paper **FishNetBot: An IoT and Deep Learning-Based Automated Fish Sorting Robot** is now published in *Array*.

FishNetBot combines computer vision, IoT communication, and robotic actuation for fish grading and sorting. Embedded hardware handles image acquisition and sensor coordination, while a cloud server performs deep learning inference. The prototype also uses ultrasonic sensors and load cells to estimate fish dimensions and weight.

This work brings together AI and hardware to explore affordable automation for fish processing.

Co-authors: Tarek Rahman, M. D. Redwan Rahman Ornob, Miftahul Jannat, Fahim Hafiz, and Md. Shafqat Talukder.
